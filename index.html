03 / HIRE A HACKER

Test your defenses.
Not your boundaries.

05 / ASSET TRACING

Follow the records.
Respect the law.

Asset tracing can involve reviewing public records, corporate filings, court documents and other lawfully accessible information to identify leads. We organize sources and timelines so clients and their legal advisers can assess the next step. We do not guarantee recovery, access private financial accounts, or represent research as proof of ownership without corroboration.

06 / CRYPTO INVESTIGATIONS

Make complex flows
easier to assess.

Public blockchain analysis can help map visible transactions, cluster potential relationships with appropriate caveats and preserve relevant transaction identifiers. We can help organize scam reports, wallet addresses, timestamps and supporting records for exchange compliance teams, law enforcement or qualified counsel. Blockchain attribution is probabilistic and must be independently verified; we never promise to hack wallets or reverse transactions.
